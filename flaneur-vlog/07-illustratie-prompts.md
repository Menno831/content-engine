# 07: Storyboard, beeldprompts (cinematische frames)

> Regi, dit zijn de beeldprompts waarmee we een visueel storyboard genereren: een reeks cinematische frames in volgorde, zodat je precies ziet hoe de film eruit gaat zien voordat we draaien. Het zijn AI-moodframes om de look en de opbouw te tonen, niet de uiteindelijke beelden. We zetten ze straks allemaal onder elkaar in één document.

## Hoe te gebruiken

1. Plak één prompt per keer in de beeld-tool (Higgsfield / GPT-image).
2. De **STIJL** zit al voorin elke prompt verwerkt, zodat alle frames op elkaar lijken en als één storyboard ogen.
3. Gebruik aspect ratio **16:9**.
4. Genereer in volgorde (Cold open → Beweging 1 → Beweging 2 → Beweging 3 → Outro), dan loopt het als een echt storyboard.

## Vaste stijl (zit in elke prompt)

`cinematic film still, photorealistic, documentary founder-vlog aesthetic in the style of high-end YouTube creators, shallow depth of field, natural light, soft warm-and-teal color grade, 16:9 widescreen, no on-screen text, no logos, generic man in his early 30s (not a specific person)`

> Tip: genereer eerst SC 1.3a (de founder die in de lens praat) als ijkbeeld. Bevalt de look, hou dan exact dezelfde stijlzin aan voor de rest.

## COLD OPEN: de inzet

**SC 0.1: montage, de inzet**
```
cinematic film still, photorealistic, shallow depth of field, soft warm-and-teal grade, 16:9. A fast-cut energy frame: a Dubai skyline at golden sunrise seen through a high window. Sense of scale and ambition, slightly tense mood, no text. Documentary founder-vlog look.
```

**SC 0.2: founder kijkt uit het raam**
```
cinematic film still, photorealistic, shallow depth of field, soft warm grade, 16:9. A man in his early 30s seen from behind, standing at a floor-to-ceiling window looking out over a Dubai skyline at sunrise. Calm, contemplative, cinematic backlight. Founder-vlog aesthetic.
```

**SC 0.3: titelkaart-moment**
```
cinematic film still, photorealistic, moody, 16:9. An empty cinematic establishing frame of a luxury penthouse interior at dawn, clean negative space in the center where a title could sit, soft volumetric morning light. No text. Founder-vlog aesthetic.
```

## BEWEGING 1: Dubai, de nieuwe basis

**SC 1.1a: establishing woning**
```
cinematic film still, photorealistic, 16:9, drone/wide aerial. A modern luxury penthouse or villa exterior with large glass windows, Dubai skyline behind, warm morning sun and light haze. Establishing, expensive, calm. Founder-vlog look.
```

**SC 1.1b: rondleiding door de woning**
```
cinematic film still, photorealistic, shallow depth of field, 16:9. A man in his early 30s walking through a minimalist high-end open-plan interior, gimbal-follow feel, soft natural light, motion energy. Documentary founder-vlog aesthetic.
```

**SC 1.1c: detailshots ochtendroutine**
```
cinematic film still, photorealistic, macro, shallow depth of field, 16:9. Quiet morning detail: a coffee cup with steam on a sleek counter, soft window light, city blurred in the background. B-roll mood, warm grade.
```

**SC 1.2a: aan het bureau**
```
cinematic film still, photorealistic, 16:9. A man in his early 30s sitting down at a clean modern home-office desk, opening a laptop, large window with a city skyline behind him, soft daylight. Calm command-center feel.
```

**SC 1.2b: over-the-shoulder dashboards**
```
cinematic film still, photorealistic, shallow depth of field, 16:9. Over-the-shoulder shot of a man looking at a laptop showing dashboards and a calendar, screen content soft/illegible, focused mood, warm desk light. Founder-vlog look.
```

**SC 1.2c: videocall met Amsterdam**
```
cinematic film still, photorealistic, 16:9. A man at his desk on a video call, a grid of team members softly visible on the laptop screen, engaged expression, natural light. Documentary aesthetic.
```

**SC 1.3a: founder reflecteert (anker-moment)**
```
cinematic film still, photorealistic, shallow depth of field, 16:9. A man in his early 30s sitting on a sofa or at his desk, looking directly into the camera, calm and honest, soft cinematic key light, blurred luxury interior behind. Intimate interview look. This is the visual anchor of the film.
```

**SC 1.3b: stilte-beeld**
```
cinematic film still, photorealistic, 16:9, wide. A small figure of a man seen from behind looking out over a city skyline at dusk, lots of empty space, contemplative, soft grade. A breathing-room shot.
```

## BEWEGING 2: Amsterdam, de machine draait

**SC 2.1a: harde cut naar Amsterdam**
```
cinematic film still, photorealistic, 16:9. Exterior of an Amsterdam canal-side office building, grey-blue daylight, a clear contrast to the warm Dubai frames. Documentary establishing shot.
```

**SC 2.1b: establishing kantoor met drukte**
```
cinematic film still, photorealistic, 16:9. Wide establishing of a busy modern office seen through large windows, many people working, sense of energy and motion, cooler grade than Dubai. Founder-vlog look.
```

**SC 2.2a: founder loopt binnen**
```
cinematic film still, photorealistic, shallow depth of field, 16:9. POV/following shot of a man in his early 30s walking into a busy open-plan office, colleagues greeting him, energy and movement, natural office light.
```

**SC 2.2b: B-roll team aan het werk**
```
cinematic film still, photorealistic, 16:9. A busy creative office: people at desks with screens, sticky notes on a glass wall, a small standing huddle, sense of momentum. B-roll energy, cooler grade.
```

**SC 2.2c: korte interactie met teamleden**
```
cinematic film still, photorealistic, shallow depth of field, 16:9. A founder talking and laughing with one or two team members at a desk, candid natural moment, warm human feel. Documentary look.
```

**SC 2.3a: rollout uitgelegd bij whiteboard**
```
cinematic film still, photorealistic, 16:9. A man in his early 30s standing next to a glass whiteboard or large screen, mid-gesture explaining a plan, a soft-focus timeline visible behind him. Confident, in-command mood.
```

**SC 2.3b: B-roll schermen/proces (teaser)**
```
cinematic film still, photorealistic, macro, shallow depth of field, 16:9. Close-up of screens showing a partly hidden website layout, deliberately soft and mysterious (not revealed), hands gesturing toward it. Teaser mood.
```

**SC 2.4a: fulfillment center (optioneel)**
```
cinematic film still, photorealistic, 16:9, wide. A man in his early 30s walking through a large fulfillment warehouse with tall shelves and stacks of boxes, a sense of huge scale, industrial light. Founder-perspective look.
```

## BEWEGING 3: de gok / climax

**SC 3.1: toon-omslag, avondlicht**
```
cinematic film still, photorealistic, 16:9. A man in his early 30s sitting alone in the evening, dark sky and city lights out the window, quieter and slightly tense mood, moody low-key light. The calm before the launch.
```

**SC 3.2: spanningsmontage / countdown**
```
cinematic film still, photorealistic, 16:9. A tense montage-style frame: a focused face lit by screen glow, a sense of countdown and last checks, motion and urgency, high-contrast moody grade.
```

**SC 3.3: de drempel vóór de rollout (cliffhanger)**
```
cinematic film still, photorealistic, 16:9. A man's hand hovering near a keyboard about to launch, dramatic focused light, held tension, the moment right before going live. Cliffhanger energy, the frame ends here.
```

---

## OUTRO: de cliffhanger

**SC 4.1: recht in de lens, de belofte**
```
cinematic film still, photorealistic, shallow depth of field, 16:9. A man in his early 30s centered, looking straight into the camera, calm and confident, soft cinematic light, making a promise to the viewer. Intimate, direct.
```

**SC 4.2: laatste beeld + CTA**
```
cinematic film still, photorealistic, 16:9, wide. A final peaceful shot: an empty desk or a city skyline view at dusk, soft warm grade, space for a subtle subscribe prompt. Calm, founder-toned ending. No text.
```

---

> **Consistentie:** wil je later jou herkenbaar in beeld in plaats van een generieke man, dan kan dat met een getrainde reference in Higgsfield. Voor een storyboard dat de look en opbouw laat zien werkt een neutrale figuur prima en snel.
