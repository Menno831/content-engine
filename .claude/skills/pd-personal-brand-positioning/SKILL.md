# Personal Brand Positioning (Deep Market Intelligence Edition)

Most people creating content are invisible because they never took the time to figure out WHO they are to the market. This skill fixes that. It combines structured discovery with deep live market intelligence — competitor mapping, audience language mining, trend velocity, gap scoring — to deliver a positioning that is provably defensible because it's backed by real data.

**Core belief:** Positioning is being the obvious choice for one specific kind of person solving one specific kind of problem. Distinctiveness + relevance. Every claim in the positioning must be grounded in evidence from the market.

**What this skill does NOT do:**
- It does NOT write titles, thumbnails, hooks, or scripts.
- It does NOT build a 30-day content calendar.
- It hands off to downstream content skills once positioning is locked.

---

## THE FOUR PHASES

**Phase 0 — Discovery:** Surface who the user actually is
**Phase 1 — Deep Market Intelligence:** Full research across every major platform
**Phase 2 — Intersection Analysis & Scoring:** Cross-reference user + market, score opportunities 1-100
**Phase 3 — Positioning Synthesis:** Deliver the brief
**Phase 4 — HTML Report:** Dark-mode visual dashboard of everything above

Do NOT skip phases.

## PHASE 0: DISCOVERY QUESTIONNAIRE

Ask one at a time (or in batches of 2-3). Wait for response. Push for specifics.

### 1. Industry / Niche
*"What industry or space do you want to build your personal brand in? Be as specific as you can — not just 'fitness' but 'strength training for women over 40.' If you're not sure, give me 2-3 guesses and we'll pressure test them."*

### 2. Background & Unfair Advantage
*"Walk me through the last 2-3 chapters of your career or life. Jobs, skills, industries, things you've built. The stuff that makes you DIFFERENT from someone who just decided yesterday to be a creator in this space."*

### 3. Character & Personality
*"If your closest friends described your personality in 3 words, what would they say? Intense? Funny? Analytical? Contrarian? Your personality IS part of your brand — don't sanitize this."*

### 4. Results You've Produced
*"What specific results have you produced — for yourself, clients, employers, students? Real numbers. Real outcomes. Even small ones if they're concrete. This is the credibility layer."*

### 5. Passions & Obsessions
*"What could you talk about for 4 hours straight without getting bored? What do you read about when nobody's asking? Obsessions = unfair advantage."*

### 6. What You Stand For (And Against)
*"What do you believe about this industry that most people get wrong? What's the conventional wisdom you disagree with? Contrarian beliefs + strong convictions are where sharp positioning lives."*

### 7. Dream Audience
*"Who specifically do you want to reach? Describe them like a real person. Age, stage of life, current situation, what they struggle with. If you say 'anyone who wants X,' push further."*

### 8. Constraints & Non-Negotiables
*"What are you NOT willing to do? Not on camera? Certain clients only? Avoid certain platforms? Any topics off limits?"*

After all answers: summarize back 4-5 bullets. Confirm before Phase 1.

## PHASE 1: DEEP MARKET INTELLIGENCE

This is the full research layer. Do NOT rely on memory — every claim must come from live searches in the current session.

### Step 1: Topic Decomposition

Before searching, break the niche into 3 layers:

- **Core topic** — the main subject
- **Sub-angles** — 4-6 specific lenses (beginner, advanced, tool-specific, fear-based, aspirational, contrarian)
- **Adjacent topics** — 3-4 related spaces that share the same audience

Write these out. They become your search matrix.

### Step 2: YouTube Intelligence

Run 6-8 searches on the topic + sub-angles. For each search:
- Top 8-10 videos: title, views, channel name, subscriber count, days since posted
- Calculate `views_per_day` for each
- Flag outliers (videos performing 3x+ the channel's average)

Pull:
- Dominant angles / content formats currently winning
- Content age of top performers (fresh vs stale = gap signal)
- Channels that are big but weak quality (low bar to beat)
- Channels that dominate with one specific angle (positioning proof)

### Step 3: Reddit Intelligence

Run Google searches to surface Reddit threads:
```
site:reddit.com "[topic]" frustration 2025 2026
site:reddit.com "[topic]" "best" OR "recommend" 2025 2026
site:reddit.com "[topic]" "doesn't work" OR "tried everything" 2025 2026
site:reddit.com "[topic]" most upvoted
```

Extract:
- 5-10 verbatim pain quotes (DIRECT quotes, with upvote counts if visible)
- 3-5 things people are sick of hearing
- 3-5 questions asked repeatedly with no good answer
- Active subreddits in the niche

### Step 4: X / Twitter Intelligence

```
site:x.com "[topic]" 2026
[topic] "changed my mind" OR "hot take"
[topic] "thread" viral
```

Pull:
- Dominant voices and follower counts
- Viral threads (high engagement = proven resonance)
- Contrarian takes that stirred debate
- Emerging vocabulary nobody else is using yet

### Step 5: Google Trends + SEO

```
"[topic]" search volume 2026
[topic] rising queries
[topic] keyword difficulty
best "[topic]" 2026
```

Pull:
- Is demand rising, flat, or declining?
- Breakout search terms (early trend signal)
- Commercial intent signals (where the money lives)

### Step 6: TikTok Intelligence

```
[topic] tiktok viral 2026 views
"[topic]" tiktok trending
```

Pull:
- Dominant short-form creators + follower counts
- Formats working (talking head, screencast, trend audio)
- Age/audience signals different from YouTube

### Step 7: LinkedIn Intelligence

```
"[topic]" site:linkedin.com viral 2026
[topic] linkedin most viewed
```

Pull:
- Top posts with high impressions
- B2B / enterprise angles not covered elsewhere
- Professional creators + their gap

### Step 8: Blog + Podcast Intelligence

```
"[topic]" blog most read 2026
"[topic]" podcast episode downloads
"[topic]" newsletter popular
```

Pull:
- Topics well covered in text but not video (video gap)
- Podcast angles not on YouTube
- Newsletter audiences underserved socially

### Step 9: Competitor & Key Voice Mapping

Build a competitor table. For every major voice found:

| Creator | Platform | Followers/Subs | Owns (what they're known for) | Misses (what they don't cover) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Name] | [Platform] | [Number] | [Angle] | [Gap] |

Answer these:
1. Who are the top 5 voices on YouTube in this space? What do they NOT cover?
2. Top 5 voices on Reddit/X/LinkedIn — do they have YouTube presence?
3. Is there someone dominating text but weak on video? (First-mover opportunity)
4. Who wins despite weak content quality? (Low bar to beat)

### Step 10: Trend Velocity Assessment

Rate the niche on a single trajectory:
- **Rising fast** — surge of new entrants, emerging vocabulary, breakout search terms
- **Growing steadily** — consistent demand, room for more voices
- **Mature plateau** — demand high but saturated
- **Declining** — avoid

Support the call with data. Name the "best window to act" (now / 3-6 months / already late).

### Step 11: Monetization Signals

```
"[topic]" sponsorship 2026
"[topic]" affiliate program
brands sponsoring "[topic]" creators
```

Assess:
- **Sponsorship activity** (high/medium/low)
- **Audience buying power** (evidence of what they spend on)
- **Affiliate potential**
- **CPM estimate** (B2B = high, B2C entertainment = lower)

### Step 12: Demand + Supply Scorecard (THE QUANTITATIVE ANCHOR)

This is the single most important step for someone running this skill themselves. After all the qualitative platform research, distill the findings into a per-keyword scorecard for the top **6-8 positioning keyword candidates**. This gives defensible, numeric proof of demand and supply for every positioning angle.

**Only use free, automatable sources. No paid tools. No logins.**

#### The 4 Data Sources

**Source 1 — Google Trends (Demand Direction)**
Navigate to `https://trends.google.com/trends/explore?q=<keyword>&date=today%2012-m`.
Pull:
- **Interest Index Peak** (0-100 relative score over last 12 months)
- **12-Month Trajectory**: Rising ↑ / Flat → / Falling ↓
- **Breakout Related Queries** (queries with +100% growth or flagged "Breakout")

**Source 2 — YouTube Top-10 View Sum (Absolute Demand)**
Search the keyword on `youtube.com/results?search_query=<keyword>`. Read the top 10 video results. Sum their view counts.

| Sum of Top-10 Views | Demand Read |
|---|---|
| 50M+ | Massive demand |
| 5M–50M | Strong demand |
| 500K–5M | Moderate demand |
| Under 500K | Niche or cold |

**Source 3 — YouTube Top-10 Competition (Supply)**
From the same top 10, record:
- **Avg channel size** (mean of top 10 subscriber counts)
- **Avg content age** (months since posted — median)
- **Creator concentration** (do 1-2 channels own 3+ of the top 10? If yes, saturated)

Rule of thumb:
- Big channels (500K+ avg) + fresh content (<6 mo) = saturated red ocean
- Small channels (<100K avg) OR old content (2+ years) = gap worth exploiting
- High concentration (1-2 dominant voices) = room to become the #3 voice

**Source 4 — Reddit Thread Volume (Cross-Platform Demand Signal)**
Google search: `site:reddit.com "<keyword>" 2025 OR 2026`.
Count threads from the last 90 days. Record top 3 upvote counts.

| Reddit Threads (90d) | Signal |
|---|---|
| 50+ | Hot active topic |
| 10–50 | Active discussion |
| Under 10 | Cold or post-peak |

#### The Scorecard Table (MANDATORY OUTPUT)

Deliver a table with exactly these columns:

| Keyword | Trends Index | Trends Direction | YT Top-10 Views | Avg Channel Size | Avg Content Age | Reddit 90d Threads | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [keyword 1] | 72 | ↑ Rising | 18.4M | 450K | 14 mo | 34 | Strong demand, moderate supply gap |
| [keyword 2] | 45 | → Flat | 3.2M | 85K | 22 mo | 9 | Niche but underserved |

#### Using the Scorecard to Ground Phase 2 Scoring

With scorecard data in hand, Phase 2's Demand and Blue Ocean scores become defensible:

**Demand Score (1-100)** = weighted blend:
- Google Trends index × 0.30
- YouTube top-10 view sum (percentile across your candidates) × 0.40
- Reddit 90d thread count (percentile) × 0.30

**Blue Ocean Score (1-100)** = inverse blend (higher = less competition):
- Avg channel size percentile (bigger = more saturated, so invert) × 0.50
- Content age (older avg = more room, so invert) × 0.30
- Creator concentration (1-2 voices dominating = opportunity for 3rd voice) × 0.20

**Rule:** Every Phase 2 Opportunity Score must be traceable to at least 3 scorecard data points. No vibes-based scoring.

## PHASE 2: INTERSECTION ANALYSIS & 1-100 SCORING

Now cross-reference Phase 0 (who the user is) with Phase 1 (the market).

### The Five-Filter Test

For each gap/opportunity, run it through:

1. **Credibility** — Does the user's background give them authority to own this angle?
2. **Personality match** — Does this angle fit their character?
3. **Values alignment** — Does it match what they believe?
4. **Passion sustainability** — Could they make content on this for years?
5. **Audience match** — Does the audience this serves match their dream audience?

### The 1-100 Scoring System

For each candidate positioning angle, score three dimensions out of 100:

**Demand Score (1-100)**
How hungry is the market?
- 0-25: Niche demand, low search volume
- 26-50: Moderate demand, some activity
- 51-75: Strong demand, consistent search and engagement
- 76-100: High-intensity demand, viral threads, high search volume

**Blue Ocean Score (1-100)**
How uncontested is the space? (Higher = less competition)
- 0-25: Red ocean — hyper saturated, dominant voices everywhere
- 26-50: Competitive but room for differentiation
- 51-75: Moderately uncontested, few credible voices
- 76-100: True blue ocean — almost nobody owns this yet

**Credibility Fit (1-100)**
How naturally can this user own the angle based on Phase 0?
- 0-25: Weak fit — user has limited credentials here
- 26-50: Reasonable fit — some background applies
- 51-75: Strong fit — directly matches background
- 76-100: Perfect fit — user is uniquely positioned to own this

### Final Opportunity Score (1-100)

Weighted formula:
```
Opportunity Score = (Demand × 0.35) + (Blue Ocean × 0.35) + (Credibility × 0.30)
```

Interpretation:
- **85-100** = Elite opportunity. Go all-in.
- **70-84** = Strong opportunity. Priority pillar.
- **55-69** = Viable. Could be a secondary angle.
- **40-54** = Caution. Re-evaluate credibility or demand.
- **Below 40** = Skip.

Score at least 4-6 candidate positions. Rank them.

## PHASE 3: POSITIONING SYNTHESIS

Deliver the brief. Use this exact structure.

### 🎯 POSITIONING STATEMENT

One sentence capturing who the user is, who they serve, what they do differently.

Format:
> "I help [specific audience] achieve [specific outcome] through [specific unique approach]."

### 🧲 THE HOOK PHRASE

The sentence someone would say to describe the user to a friend. Under 15 words. Sticky.

### 🏛️ THREE CONTENT PILLARS

The 3 ideas the user becomes known for. For each:
- **Name** (2-4 words)
- **Definition** (1 sentence)
- **Why it wins** (tied to specific market gap from Phase 1)
- **Opportunity Score** (from Phase 2, out of 100)

### 🌊 BLUE OCEAN OPPORTUNITIES (Ranked)

3-5 positioning angles the user could own. For each:
- **The opportunity**
- **Opportunity Score (1-100)**
- **Demand / Blue Ocean / Credibility sub-scores**
- **Why it's a blue ocean** (specific market gap evidence)
- **Competitor gap** (who exists here and what they miss)

### 🚩 RED FLAGS — ANGLES TO AVOID

2-3 angles to stay away from, with specific reasons.

### 🗣️ NAMING ASSOCIATIONS

5-10 terms/concepts the user should be mentally linked to.

### 💬 AUDIENCE LANGUAGE MAP

From Phase 1 Reddit/X/forum research, deliver verbatim quotes in three categories:
- **Pain language** — what they say when frustrated
- **Aspiration language** — what they want
- **Question language** — what they're asking with no good answer

### 🏆 COMPETITOR TABLE

From Phase 1 Step 9, the full competitor map (Creator / Platform / Followers / Owns / Misses).

### 📈 TREND VELOCITY

Single-line verdict (rising/steady/mature/declining) + data-backed rationale + best window to act.

### 💰 MONETIZATION SIGNALS

Sponsorship activity, audience buying power, affiliate potential, CPM estimate.

### ⚡ DIFFERENTIATION PLAYBOOK

Side-by-side:
- **What everyone else is doing** (the pattern)
- **What nobody is doing** (the gap)
- **The contrarian angle** (opposing take that could go viral)
- **The underserved audience** (who existing creators ignore)

### 💡 SEED CONTENT IDEAS (by pillar)

2-3 raw topic seeds per pillar. Topic only, no titles, no hooks.

### 🧭 IDENTITY STATEMENT

3-5 sentence paragraph paste-ready for bio, LinkedIn about, or video intro.

- Subtle borders: `rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`
- Strong borders: `rgba(255,255,255,0.12)`

**Typography:**
- Stack: `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "SF Pro Display", "Inter", system-ui, sans-serif`
- Hero headline: `clamp(44px, 7vw, 88px)`, weight 600, letter-spacing -0.035em, line-height 1.02
- Section headlines: `clamp(32px, 4.5vw, 56px)`, weight 600, letter-spacing -0.025em
- Subheads: 19-24px, weight 400, color `#a1a1a6`

- Eyebrow: 13px, weight 600, letter-spacing 0.08em, uppercase, color `#a1a1a6`
- Big numbers/scores: weight 600, letter-spacing -0.04em

**Layout:**
- Max content width: 1080px, centered
- Section padding: 120px top/bottom desktop, 80px mobile
- Card radius: 16px
- Subtle shadow: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px rgba(255,255,255,0.06)`
- Generous whitespace — never cramped

**4. Demand + Supply Scorecard (THE DATA TABLE)**
- Full-width dark table section, generous padding
- Section eyebrow: "DEMAND + SUPPLY"

  - Green (`#30d158`) for high demand / strong gap signals
  - Accent blue (`#0a84ff`) for neutral

- 3-column layout (stacks mobile): Pain / Aspiration / Question

**8. Blue Ocean Opportunities — Ranked Scorecard**

**13. Seed Content Ideas**
- Grouped by pillar
